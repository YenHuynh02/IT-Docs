# IT Audit Overview:

Definition and objectives of IT audits.

Legal basis and audit types (financial, performance, compliance).

Process stages (preparation, understanding, risk evaluation, execution, reporting).

# Standards and Frameworks:

International Standards for Supreme Audit Institutions (ISSAI).

ISACA standards for IT audit and CISA certification.

ISO standards (ISO 27000, 20000, 31000).

Key frameworks: COBIT, ITIL, TOGAF, Val IT.

# Risk-Based Auditing:

Risk evaluation to prioritize IT audit areas.

Risk IT principles and risk audit types (inherent, control, detection risks).

Audit process based on risk.

# IT Controls:

Types of controls: general vs application controls.

Examples of general controls: IT governance, security, change management, disaster recovery.

Examples of application controls: input validation, completeness checks, reconciliation.

# Planning:

Audit planning aligned with audit goals and risks.

Defining the audit scope based on risk assessment and system importance.

Resource allocation and budgeting.

# IT Governance:

Components: IT strategy, structure, processes, and responsibilities.

Importance for IT audits: effective governance supports reliable controls and audit results.

System Development and Maintenance:

Focus on procurement, development lifecycle, system changes, and maintenance audits.

# IT Operations and KPIs:

Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) for assessing IT operations efficiency (e.g., system uptime, response times).

Auditing operational effectiveness in meeting service, security, and business goals.

# Information Security:

Security audit focus: protecting data and systems from unauthorized access and disruptions.

Evaluating information security according to the CIA triad (Confidentiality, Integrity, Availability).

# Change Management:

Ensuring system changes are recorded, approved, tested, and implemented properly.

Business Continuity and Disaster Recovery:

Ensuring IT systems remain operational during disasters.

Evaluating disaster recovery plans.

Outsourcing and Third-Party Risks:

Assessing risks associated with outsourcing IT functions.

Auditing third-party providers for compliance and security.

ERP System Audits:

Auditing ERP systems to ensure they support business processes, security, and compliance.

# Reporting:

Documenting findings, conclusions, and recommendations clearly for management.
